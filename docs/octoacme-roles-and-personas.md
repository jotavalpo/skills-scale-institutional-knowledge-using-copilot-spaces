# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers define user experience and interface direction so solutions are usable, accessible, and consistent with product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Create and iterate on user flows, wireframes, and UI mockups
- Validate usability and accessibility early in the lifecycle
- Collaborate with Product Managers on discovery and acceptance criteria
- Partner with Developers on implementation-ready design specs
- Support Project Managers with design scope and sequencing inputs

### Goals
- Reduce usability risks before development starts
- Improve task completion and user satisfaction
- Keep design decisions aligned with product outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers
- Annotated wireframes/prototypes linked in backlog items
- Feedback sessions from demos and usability checks

### Key Interactions / Handoffs
- To Product Managers: discovery insights, user flows, and prioritized design options
- To Developers: implementation-ready wireframes, components, and interaction specs
- To Project Managers: design milestones, dependencies, and design-risk flags

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts provide measurement frameworks and analysis so Product Managers and Project Managers can make informed delivery and product decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and validate project and feature-level KPIs with Product Managers
- Build and maintain analytics dashboards and tracking definitions
- Analyze adoption, quality, and operational trends during execution
- Support Developers with instrumentation requirements and event validation
- Provide Project Managers with trend-based risks and progress signals

### Goals
- Improve confidence in prioritization and release decisions
- Detect issues and opportunities early through metrics
- Ensure success criteria are measurable and visible

### Typical Communication
- KPI alignment sessions with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Weekly dashboard reviews during execution
- Insight summaries tied to milestones and retrospectives

### Key Interactions / Handoffs
- To Product Managers: KPI definitions, experiment readouts, and recommendation memos
- To Developers: tracking specs and event-quality feedback
- To Project Managers: delivery dashboards and risk indicators for status reporting

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers produce and maintain clear product and process documentation that supports internal teams and end users.

### Responsibilities
- Define documentation scope for each release
- Draft and maintain user guides, internal runbooks, and release notes
- Review technical changes with Developers for accuracy
- Align documentation priorities with Product Managers and release scope
- Coordinate publication timing with Project Managers

### Goals
- Keep customer-facing and internal docs current with shipped behavior
- Reduce ambiguity and support load through clear documentation
- Ensure release communication is complete and reusable

### Typical Communication
- Documentation planning in sprint/release rituals
- Async doc reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- Pre-release publication check with Project Managers

### Key Interactions / Handoffs
- From Developers: technical implementation details and change notes
- From Product Managers: messaging priorities and feature context
- To Project Managers: documentation readiness status and publication checklist
- To Customer Success Lead: final release notes and user-facing guidance

---

## Customer Success Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success Leads represent customer needs in delivery planning and ensure successful adoption of released capabilities.

### Responsibilities
- Consolidate customer feedback and recurrent pain points
- Partner with Product Managers to prioritize adoption-impacting improvements
- Prepare enablement plans, rollout communication, and stakeholder briefings
- Coordinate with Project Managers on release readiness communications
- Provide Developers with field feedback on usability and quality issues

### Goals
- Increase feature adoption and customer satisfaction
- Shorten onboarding and support cycles after releases
- Create a feedback loop from customers into planning and retrospectives

### Typical Communication
- Regular feedback syncs with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Enablement planning with Technical Writer before release
- Customer communication updates during rollout

### Key Interactions / Handoffs
- To Product Managers: customer feedback themes and impact summaries
- To Project Managers: rollout risks, communication dependencies, and launch readiness
- To Developers: escalated usability/quality findings from customers
- From Technical Writer: release notes and guides used in enablement

---

## QA Specialist

### Role Summary
QA Specialists define and execute validation activities to ensure releases meet quality standards and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans with Developers and Project Managers
- Validate acceptance criteria and regression coverage before release
- Execute manual and automated checks for critical workflows
- Track defects and verify fixes with Developers
- Provide release readiness recommendations to Product Managers and Project Managers

### Goals
- Prevent high-impact defects from reaching production
- Improve confidence in release quality and stability
- Shorten feedback loops between build and validation

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions with Developers and Project Managers
- Defect triage with Developers and Product Managers
- Release go/no-go quality updates with Project Managers

### Key Interactions / Handoffs
- From Product Managers: acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- From Developers: testable builds and fix confirmations
- To Project Managers: test plan status, defect risk, and QA signoff decisions
- To Technical Writer: validated behavior changes for release notes accuracy
